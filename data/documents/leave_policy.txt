NOVATECH INDUSTRIES
EMPLOYEE LEAVE POLICY

1. CASUAL LEAVE

Every full-time employee is entitled to 12 days of casual leave per calendar year.

Casual leave is intended for short personal requirements and unexpected situations.

Employees should normally request casual leave at least one working day in advance.

2. SICK LEAVE

Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.

A medical certificate may be requested when sick leave exceeds three consecutive working days.

3. EARNED LEAVE

Employees receive 18 days of earned leave per calendar year.

Earned leave should normally be requested at least seven days before the intended leave period.

Unused earned leave may be carried forward up to a maximum accumulated balance of 30 days.

4. LEAVE APPROVAL

Leave requests must be submitted through the ERP leave management system.

The employee's reporting manager is responsible for approving or rejecting the request.

Employees should not assume that a leave request is approved until its status is shown as approved in the ERP system.

5. UNPAID LEAVE

Employees who have exhausted applicable paid leave may request unpaid leave.

Unpaid leave requires approval from both the reporting manager and Human Resources.

6. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS

Company-declared public holidays are separate from employee leave balances and do not reduce casual, sick, or earned leave balances.
