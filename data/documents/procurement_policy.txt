NOVATECH INDUSTRIES
PROCUREMENT POLICY

1. PURCHASE REQUESTS

Employees requiring goods or services for official purposes must raise a purchase request through the ERP procurement module.

The request should contain the product or service description, required quantity, estimated cost, business justification, and preferred delivery date.

2. PURCHASE APPROVAL

Purchase requests below INR 25,000 require approval from the department manager.

Purchases between INR 25,000 and INR 100,000 require approval from both the department manager and procurement manager.

Purchases above INR 100,000 require approval from the finance head in addition to department and procurement approval.

3. VENDOR SELECTION

For purchases exceeding INR 50,000, procurement should normally obtain quotations from at least three eligible vendors.

Vendor selection should consider price, product quality, delivery performance, warranty, service quality, and previous vendor performance.

4. PURCHASE ORDERS

Approved procurement requests must be converted into purchase orders before goods or services are ordered.

Every purchase order must contain a unique purchase order number.

Purchase orders must specify the vendor, goods or services, quantity, agreed price, delivery details, and payment terms.

5. EMERGENCY PROCUREMENT

Emergency purchases may follow an expedited approval process when delaying the purchase could disrupt essential business operations.

The reason for emergency procurement must be documented in the ERP system.

6. CONFLICT OF INTEREST

Employees involved in procurement must disclose personal or financial relationships with vendors.

Employees must not accept unauthorized gifts or financial benefits from suppliers.
