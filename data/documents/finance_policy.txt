NOVATECH INDUSTRIES
FINANCE AND INVOICE POLICY

1. CUSTOMER INVOICES

Customer invoices must be recorded in the ERP finance system using a unique invoice number.

Each invoice should contain the customer name, invoice amount, invoice date, payment due date, and payment status.

2. PAYMENT TERMS

The standard customer payment term is 30 days from the invoice date unless different terms have been approved in the customer agreement.

3. UNPAID INVOICES

Invoices that have not yet been paid but have not crossed their payment due date should remain classified as unpaid.

The finance department is responsible for monitoring unpaid invoices.

4. OVERDUE INVOICES

An invoice is considered overdue once its payment due date has passed and payment has not been received.

The finance team should contact customers with overdue invoices and record follow-up activities.

5. EXPENSE APPROVAL

Employee business expenses below INR 10,000 require approval from the reporting manager.

Expenses of INR 10,000 or more require additional finance approval.

6. FINANCIAL RECORDS

Financial information stored in the ERP system should be accurate, complete, and supported by appropriate documentation.

Unauthorized alteration of financial records is prohibited.
