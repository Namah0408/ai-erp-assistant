NOVATECH INDUSTRIES
INVENTORY MANAGEMENT POLICY

1. INVENTORY RECORDING

All inventory items purchased by NovaTech Industries must be recorded in the ERP inventory management system.

Each product should have a unique product code, product description, category, supplier information, quantity, price, and reorder level.

2. STOCK MONITORING

Inventory quantities should be updated whenever stock is received, issued, returned, damaged, or otherwise adjusted.

Department managers are responsible for reporting inventory discrepancies.

3. REORDER LEVEL

Every regularly consumed inventory item should have a defined reorder level.

When the available stock quantity reaches or falls below the reorder level, procurement should evaluate whether a new purchase order is required.

4. STOCK VERIFICATION

Physical stock verification should be conducted at least once every quarter.

Differences between physical inventory and ERP inventory records must be investigated.

5. DAMAGED GOODS

Damaged or unusable goods should be identified separately and should not be included in usable stock quantities.

Disposal of damaged goods requires appropriate departmental approval.

6. INVENTORY SECURITY

Access to high-value inventory storage areas should be restricted to authorized personnel.

Inventory movements involving high-value items must be properly documented.
