NOVATECH INDUSTRIES
HUMAN RESOURCES POLICY

1. WORKING HOURS

The standard working hours at NovaTech Industries are from 9:30 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday.

Employees are expected to complete a minimum of eight working hours per day, excluding the lunch break.

Employees may use flexible arrival and departure timings with approval from their reporting manager.

2. PROBATION

New employees normally undergo a probation period of six months.

During probation, employee performance, attendance, conduct, and role suitability are evaluated.

The probation period may be extended based on management review.

3. PERFORMANCE REVIEWS

Formal performance reviews are conducted twice every year.

Performance is evaluated based on individual objectives, teamwork, quality of work, attendance, professional development, and contribution to organizational goals.

4. REMOTE WORK

Eligible employees may work remotely up to two days per week with approval from their reporting manager.

Employees handling activities that require physical presence may not be eligible for remote work.

5. CODE OF CONDUCT

Employees must maintain professional behavior, protect company information, avoid conflicts of interest, and follow all company security policies.

Harassment, discrimination, fraudulent activities, unauthorized disclosure of information, and misuse of company resources are strictly prohibited.

6. EMPLOYEE RECORDS

Employees must ensure that their personal and professional information stored in the ERP system remains accurate.

Changes in address, contact details, bank information, or emergency contact details should be reported to Human Resources.
